# SettleWise product

SettleWise is an agentic relocation assistant for people moving to and settling in a new city.
It helps users compare neighborhoods based on budget, commute, safety, lifestyle, and daily
needs, then produces personalized move-in plans.

Prioritize trustworthy, actionable guidance and clear trade-offs. When designing features,
consider a user's relocation context, confidence, time constraints, and privacy.
